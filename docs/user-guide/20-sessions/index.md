# Work in your session

This section tells you how OpenTacit operates while you work in your AI tools.
It covers suggestions during relevant work, playbook searches, contributions,
and the evidence recorded for each technique.

Read **How OpenTacit behaves in your tools** first for an overview. The other
chapters give the details of each task.

- [How OpenTacit behaves in your tools](03-how-tacit-behaves-in-your-harness.md) —
  an overview: the surfaces that OpenTacit reaches, what runs automatically, how
  to speak to OpenTacit, where it can show output, and what stays private.
- [Work with suggestions](04-work-with-suggestions.md) — the ◆ OpenTacit block,
  the actions you can do with it, the `@tacit` advisor, and the status line.
- [Search and review on demand](05-search-and-review.md) — how to search,
  review, and audit the playbook, get usage data, and review drafts without
  the dashboard.
- [Pause or leave](08-pause-or-leave.md) — stop being observed for an hour,
  disconnect this machine, or remove a registry, and what each one leaves
  behind.
- [Contribute a technique](06-contribute-a-technique.md) — make a technique
  from a move that worked, and propose changes to techniques that exist.
- [Give feedback](07-give-feedback.md) — how OpenTacit records adoption and
  helped signals, and what data leaves your machine.

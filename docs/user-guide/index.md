# OpenTacit User Guide

![OpenTacit](images/hero.svg)

OpenTacit helps you use AI better.

Models can do much more than most people ask of them. That gap between what a
model can do and what its users try is called *capability overhang*. It is
growing with the extremely rapid improvements in what models can do.

Capability overhang persists because the best ways to work with AI aren't
easily shared. Power users get far more out of the same model—around seven
times more, by one measure. The best AI users know what context to hand it,
which tools to give it, and which steps suit a particular job.

That knowledge is *tacit*: known to the most advanced users, but unavailable to
everyone else. OpenTacit captures it automatically, updates it when conditions
and models change, and shares it right at the moment it is most needed. It
watches how you work with your AI tools, determines what works best, and
collects it as reusable techniques in a shared playbook. Then it offers those
techniques back to you when the work calls for them.

Use OpenTacit *on your own* and it shows you how you work with models and
agents, carries your best techniques across every agent you use, maintains them,
and retires the ones you or your models have outgrown.

*Share it with colleagues* and it does much more, surfacing the best techniques
from their collective human intelligence. Over time your OpenTacit playbook
describes your business: its people, its processes, and the way they work.

This guide covers OpenTacit day to day: how to set it up, how to work with
suggestions in your AI sessions, and how to run the registry that your
organization shares.

## Where to start

- **You are trying OpenTacit on your own.** Start with
  [Start on your own](10-get-started/00-start-on-your-own.md). Two commands, and
  it ends with a technique in your next session.
- **Your organization already runs OpenTacit.** Start with
  [Join your organization's registry](10-get-started/03-join-your-organization.md), then read
  [Work with suggestions in your session](20-sessions/04-work-with-suggestions.md).
- **You set up OpenTacit for your organization.** Start with
  [Set up a registry](10-get-started/02-set-up-a-registry.md).
- **You are new to OpenTacit.** Read [OpenTacit at a glance](10-get-started/01-tacit-at-a-glance.md)
  first. It explains the ideas that the rest of the guide uses.

## Read this guide anywhere

This guide is part of the registry itself. In the dashboard, click the account
button in the top-right corner. Then select User Guide. The same files are in
the repository in `docs/user-guide/`. Thus you can also read them on GitHub or
in your editor.

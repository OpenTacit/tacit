# OpenTacit User Guide

![OpenTacit](images/hero.svg)

OpenTacit records which ways of working with AI help your organization. It saves
validated methods as techniques in the organization's playbook and suggests
them to other members when they apply. Each suggestion includes its measured
results.

One person can start in one repository. OpenTacit turns written conventions into
techniques and suggests them during sessions. This requires no colleagues,
server, or model key. An organization uses the same process with results from
more members.

Over time, the playbook reflects your business and how its people, processes,
and systems work. OpenTacit runs in the tools that your colleagues already use and
suggests techniques during their work.

This guide shows you how to use OpenTacit each day. It shows how to set up OpenTacit
and how to work with suggestions in your coding sessions. It also shows how
to run the registry that your organization shares.

## Where to start

- **You are trying OpenTacit on your own.** Start with
  [Start on your own](10-get-started/00-start-on-your-own.md). Two commands, and
  it ends with a technique in your next session.
- **Your organization already runs OpenTacit.** Start with
  [Join your organization's registry](10-get-started/03-join-your-organization.md), then read
  [Work with suggestions in your session](20-sessions/04-work-with-suggestions.md).
- **You set up OpenTacit for your organization.** Start with
  [Set up a registry](10-get-started/02-set-up-a-registry.md).
- **You are new to OpenTacit.** Read [OpenTacit at a glance](10-get-started/01-tacit-at-a-glance.md)
  first. It explains the ideas that the rest of the guide uses.

This guide is part of the registry itself. In the dashboard, click the account

the repository in `docs/user-guide/`. Thus you can also read them on GitHub or
in your editor.

The `tacit` command is not the mark. You may keep, rename, or wrap it under the
software license. The same applies to these product identifiers: the
`TACIT_*` environment variables, the `X-Tacit-*` headers, the MCP tool names and
the repository path. `internal/product` keeps these identifiers stable.

## You do not need permission to

- Use the software, for anything, including commercially.
- Say your product works with OpenTacit, integrates with OpenTacit, or is built
  on OpenTacit — as long as the wording describes a relationship and does not
  imply we published, endorsed or support it.
- Redistribute unmodified builds under the name OpenTacit, including packaging
  them for a distribution, a registry or an internal artifact store.
- Use the name in writing: articles, talks, courses, comparisons, criticism,
  training material, and in the ordinary reference sense throughout your own
  documentation.
- Implement the published schemas and wire contracts and say so — "implements
  the OpenTacit technique schema" is accurate and welcome. The schemas are an
  open specification and independent implementations are the point.
- Run a modified build for your own organization, under any name, including ours.

## Please do

- Rename a fork you distribute publicly. Pick your own name and describe it as
  "a fork of OpenTacit". Users need to know whose measurement semantics they are
  getting when the funnel numbers reach a procurement conversation.
- Use "OpenTacit" as an adjective on a noun of your own — "the OpenTacit
  registry", not "an OpenTacit" — and write it as one word, both capitals.
- Make the origin obvious in any redistribution: link back to the upstream
  project.

## Please do not

- Use the name or logo for your company, product, service or domain in a way
  that suggests it is the official one, or that we run it.
- Use it in a name that could be mistaken for this project — "OpenTacit Cloud",
  "OpenTacit Enterprise", "OpenTacit Pro", "OpenTacitAI" and the like are
  reserved.
- Claim endorsement, certification, partnership or affiliation we have not
  given, and do not use the name in advertising that implies any of them.
- Modify the logo, or use it as your own icon or favicon.
- Register the mark, or a confusingly similar one, anywhere.

## Hosted and commercial use

Running OpenTacit as a paid service for others is permitted by the license and
by this policy — but name the service after yourself, not after us. "Acme
Playbook, built on OpenTacit" is fine. "OpenTacit Hosting" is not.

origin and do not cause confusion about who provides or supports them.
